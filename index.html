Important — How to Use This Tool

This tool analyses procedural and notice-level defects in income tax penalty notices under Sections 271(1)(c), 270A, and 271AAB. It does not evaluate the merits of the underlying tax addition. Even if your notice is procedurally valid, you may have strong substantive defences. Always consult a qualified Chartered Accountant or Tax Advocate before taking legal action.
