Penalty for concealment of income or furnishing inaccurate particulars. Applicable upto AY 2016-17.
Section 270A
Penalty for under-reporting or misreporting of income. Applicable from AY 2017-18 onwards.
Section 271AAB
Penalty on undisclosed income found during search proceedings under Section 132.
